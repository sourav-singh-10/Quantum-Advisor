Terms of use

Last updated 17 October 2026

Quantum Advisor is an educational project. Nothing it produces is investment advice.

It is not registered with SEBI as an investment adviser or research analyst. Before you buy or sell anything, check the facts yourself and speak to a SEBI-registered adviser.

1. What the service does

Quantum Advisor uses a team of AI agents to research NSE-listed stocks: it gathers prices, company figures and news, debates a buy, hold or sell call, and sizes it against the profile you give it. It also writes daily briefs on companies you follow and reviews holdings you upload.

2. No advice and no guarantee

  • Analyses, scores, targets, stop-losses and briefs are educational illustrations, not recommendations to deal in any security.
  • Past prices and the committee's past calls do not tell you what will happen next. You can lose money investing.
  • You alone decide what to do with your money, and you are responsible for those decisions.

3. Data and AI judgement can be wrong

  • Market data comes from free public sources. It can be delayed by up to 15 minutes, incomplete, or occasionally wrong.
  • The agents' reasoning is produced by a large language model. Guardrails, rule fallbacks and a risk check limit errors but cannot remove them. Every run keeps a trace so you can see which agent said what, and from which data.
  • Documents you attach are read by the document analyst; text in them that tries to instruct the AI is removed and reported, but you should only upload files you have the right to share.

4. Your account

  • Keep your password private. If you sign in with Google, Google confirms your email; we never see your Google password.
  • “Keep me signed in” keeps you signed in on that browser for 30 days. Do not use it on a shared computer.

5. Your data

We store your email, a hashed password (never the password itself), your investor profile and goals, your watchlist, holdings you choose to save, price alerts, and the analyses you run with their traces. They are used only to run the service for you. They are not sold or shared for advertising. Requests to the AI model include the stock and your profile, never your email or password.

6. Fair use

Do not try to break, overload or misuse the service, read other people's data, or use it to mislead others about a security. The free model tier has limits, so heavy use may slow down or switch agents to their rule-based fallbacks.
